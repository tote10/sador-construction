# Sador General Construction Company Profile

## Company Overview

Sador General Construction is a general contractor focused on delivering reliable, high-quality construction services across Ethiopia. The company combines technical capability, disciplined project control, and a strong commitment to safety and integrity.

We approach every project as a long-term responsibility, not just a short-term contract. Whether the work involves residential buildings, commercial spaces, or public infrastructure, our goal is to deliver durable results that reflect professionalism and precision.

## Our Identity

- Established: 2012 E.C.
- Founder and General Manager: Eng. Tinsae Fikadu
- Focus: Small-, medium-, and large-scale general civil construction
- Positioning: Reliable partner for quality-driven delivery

## Vision

To become the most reliable construction partner in the industry by 2030, known for high-quality service delivery, scheduled completion, and a strong safety record.

## Mission

To deliver high-quality construction services through proper resource management and skilled craftsmanship, completing projects on time and within budget while reducing waste and improving client satisfaction.

## Core Values

Our values are summarized in the SADOR principle:

- Safety First: We maintain strict on-site safety standards and training.
- Accountability: We manage resources transparently and report clearly.
- Diligence: We pay attention to detail and execute work carefully.
- Operational Excellence: We optimize workflows to reduce delay and waste.
- Reliability: We deliver consistently and stand behind our work.

## Why Clients Choose Us

- Strong accountability culture
- Proven reliability in project execution
- Organized resource control systems
- Skilled workforce with practical construction experience
- Commitment to on-time delivery and quality workmanship

## How We Work

Our project delivery process follows a structured sequence:

1. Initiation and assessment
2. Planning and resource alignment
3. Execution of construction works
4. Monitoring and quality control
5. Final completion and handover

This approach helps us keep projects organized, aligned with client expectations, and delivered within the agreed scope.
